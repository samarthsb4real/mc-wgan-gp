8. CONCLUSION
==============

We presented a systematic study of conditioning bypass in Mask-Conditioned
WGAN-GP for industrial surface defect synthesis. Starting from a generator
that completely ignored mask and class conditioning (SSIM > 0.95 across all
class pairs), we diagnosed three root causes — mask signal washout, critic
blindness, and absence of mask reconstruction accountability — and implemented
a multi-pronged architectural fix.

The fixed MC-WGAN-GP produces synthetic images with FID scores of 100–118
(2× improvement over the untrained baseline of ~215) and variance ratios
of 0.82–0.92 relative to real images, confirming that the generator learned
meaningful defect structure. The conditioning bypass bug is resolved.

However, our rigorous 5-fold cross-validation evaluation with McNemar's
statistical testing reveals that GAN augmentation does not significantly
improve classifier performance over baseline on this severely imbalanced
small dataset (p=0.832). In contrast, simple oversampling with real image
duplication significantly outperforms both baseline (p=0.0005) and GAN
augmentation (p=0.0002), achieving 96.65% ± 0.41% accuracy vs. 94.64% ±
1.92% for baseline and 94.42% ± 0.52% for GAN augmentation.

The key practical recommendation for practitioners: when minority class
sample counts are below ~100, oversampling is a more reliable augmentation
strategy than GAN synthesis. GAN augmentation requires sufficient training
data for the generator to learn diverse, class-discriminative patterns —
a condition not met by the most challenging minority classes in this dataset
(27 Fray samples, 57 Crack samples).

The negative result is itself a contribution. GAN augmentation papers
frequently report positive results without controlled ablations or
statistical significance testing, making it difficult to assess whether
improvements are due to synthetic data quality or simply increased training
set size. Our controlled comparison with an oversampling ablation and
McNemar's testing provides a methodological template for rigorous evaluation
of GAN augmentation claims.

Future work should explore: (1) transfer learning from larger industrial
datasets to improve generator quality with limited data, (2) differentiable
augmentation during GAN training to prevent critic memorization, (3)
fold-specific synthetic image generation for more rigorous cross-validation,
and (4) domain-specific FID calibration for industrial grayscale images.
